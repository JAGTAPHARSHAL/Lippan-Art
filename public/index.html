FROM THE HANDS OF KUTCH

EARTH, SHAPED WITH PATIENCE

Made of the desert.
Made for your home.

In the homes of Kutch, Lippan work began with mud and mirror: cool walls, reflected light, patterns shaped by memory. We honour that spirit in every piece we make.

Clay is pressed into small, winding forms. Mirrors catch the sun. And slowly, a flat surface becomes something with its own quiet presence.
